About Us

Our Mission

Taste of Africa exists to promote African food heritage. We celebrate the dishes, ingredients, and stories that make African cuisine rich and diverse, and we make them easy to explore for everyone.

A Brief History of African Cuisine

African food has been shaped over thousands of years by farming, trade, and migration. Grains like millet and sorghum, and crops like yam, grew across the continent long before outside influences arrived.

Trade brought new flavours. Along the East African coast, Indian Ocean trade introduced spices and rice dishes. Later, crops such as maize, cassava, and chili peppers arrived from the Americas and became everyday staples.

African Food Around the World

African cooking traditions have travelled far. Ingredients like okra and cooking methods like one-pot rice dishes shaped food in the Caribbean, Brazil, and the southern United States. Today, dishes like jollof rice and street foods like the rolex are loved well beyond Africa.

Where East Meets West

Kōzo began in Accra, Ghana, and now has a home in Kigali, Rwanda, so West and East Africa meet at one table. Its Afro-Asian menu uses local produce, including Rwandan Akabanga chili oil.
