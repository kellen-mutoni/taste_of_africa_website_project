West Africa

West African food is bold, spicy, and full of flavour. Peppers, tomatoes, and onions form the base of many dishes, and big shared meals are at the heart of celebrations.

Staples

  • Rice, cooked in rich sauces
  • Yam and cassava, boiled, fried, or pounded
  • Plantain, fried or roasted
  • Millet, maize, and black-eyed peas

Cooking Techniques

  • One-pot cooking, where rice absorbs the sauce
  • Deep frying plantain and snacks
  • Grilling spiced meat over open fire
  • Pounding yam and cassava into a smooth dough

Example Dishes

  • Jollof Rice: one-pot spiced rice from Nigeria
  • Kelewele: spiced fried plantain from Ghana
