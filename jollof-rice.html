Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is a one-pot dish loved across West Africa. Rice is cooked in a rich sauce of tomatoes, peppers, and spices until every grain is full of flavour. Nigerian jollof is a star at parties, weddings, and celebrations, and it is often served with fried plantain and chicken.

Ingredients

For the chicken

  • 3 medium chicken thighs
  • A handful of onion
  • Half a thumb-sized piece of ginger
  • 2 cloves of garlic, smashed
  • 1 teaspoon of thyme
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • 2 teaspoons of salt
  • 2 teaspoons of crushed chicken bouillon cubes
  • 1/2 cup of water

For the tomato sauce

  • 9 medium tomatoes (600 g)
  • 3 large bell peppers (550 g)
  • 1 to 2 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 1/2 small red onion
  • 1 bulb of garlic
  • A few sprigs of fresh thyme

For the rice

  • 6 cups of rice (1200 g)
  • 1/2 cup of oil
  • A handful of chopped onion
  • 12 oz (327 g) of tomato paste
  • 1 1/2 cups of blended tomato sauce
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons of salt
  • 1 teaspoon of crushed chicken bouillon cube
  • 1 tablespoon of dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tablespoon of curry powder
  • 2 cups of chicken stock
  • 2 cups of water
Cooking Instructions
  1. Season the chicken with the onion, ginger, garlic, thyme, curry powder, salt, and bouillon. Add the water and cook until done.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, scotch bonnet, red onion, garlic, and thyme into a smooth sauce.
  3. Heat the oil in a pot and fry the chopped onion until soft, then add the tomato paste and fry for a few minutes.
  4. Pour in the blended tomato sauce and cook until it thickens.
  5. Stir in the salt, bouillon, thyme, bay leaves, and curry powder, then add the stock and water and bring to a boil.
  6. Add the washed rice, cover tightly, and cook on low heat until the rice is soft. Serve with the chicken.
