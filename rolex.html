Rolex

Rolex is a popular Ugandan street food. Eggs are cooked into a soft omelette with vegetables, then rolled inside a warm chapati. The name is said to come from "rolled eggs". It is cheap, filling, and eaten at any time of day.

Ingredients

  • 2 chapatis
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • A handful of shredded cabbage
  • 2 tablespoons of cooking oil
  • Salt, to taste
Cooking Instructions
  1. Beat the eggs in a bowl and mix in the onion, tomato, cabbage, and salt.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and pour in the egg mixture.
  3. Cook until the omelette is set, then place a warm chapati on top.
  4. Flip so the chapati touches the pan for a few seconds.
  5. Roll the chapati tightly around the omelette and serve hot.
