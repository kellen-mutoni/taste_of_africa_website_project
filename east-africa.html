East Africa

East African food is hearty, simple, and built around fresh local ingredients. Meals are often shared with family and friends, and many dishes come with a starchy side and a sauce or stew.

Staples

  • Maize, cooked into a stiff porridge
  • Cassava and its leaves
  • Plantain (also called matoke)
  • Beans and sweet potatoes

Cooking Techniques

  • Steaming food wrapped in banana leaves
  • Grilling meat over charcoal
  • Slow stewing greens and beans until soft

Example Dishes

  • Isombe: pounded cassava leaves from Rwanda
  • Rolex: egg and chapati roll from Uganda
